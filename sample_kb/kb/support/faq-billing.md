# Billing FAQ

**Q: When am I charged for my subscription?**
A: Monthly plans are charged on the same calendar day each month as your
signup date. Annual plans are charged once, upfront, on the signup
anniversary.

**Q: Can I switch plans mid-cycle?**
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge for the
remainder of the current cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the
next billing cycle.

**Q: What happens if my card is declined?**
A: We retry a declined card 3 times over 5 days (day 1, day 3, day 5). If all
retries fail, the account is placed in a past-due state and API access is
restricted to read-only until payment succeeds.

**Q: How do refunds work?**
A: See our current refund policy for full details on refund windows by plan
type.

**Q: Where can I download invoices?**
A: Dashboard > Billing > Invoices. Invoices are also emailed automatically
when generated.

**Q: Do you charge sales tax / VAT?**
A: Tax is calculated automatically based on your billing address and applied
at checkout where required by local law.
