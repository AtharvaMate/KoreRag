# Account Management FAQ

**Q: How do I add a teammate to my account?**
A: Dashboard > Settings > Team > Invite. Invited teammates receive an email
with a signup link valid for 7 days.

**Q: What are the different user roles?**
A: Admin (full access, including billing and API keys), Developer (API keys
and technical settings, no billing access), and Analyst (read-only dashboard
access, no keys or settings).

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Dashboard > Settings > Danger Zone > Close Account. Account closure is
subject to a 30-day grace period during which the account can be reactivated;
after 30 days it is permanently deleted per `data-retention-policy.md`.

**Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?**
A: Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. If your account has SSO
enabled, password reset is handled by your identity provider, not by
Northwind.

**Q: Can I have multiple Northwind accounts under one company?**
A: Yes, this is common for companies running separate legal entities or
regional subsidiaries. Contact your account manager to link them for
consolidated billing.
