# Support Ticket #48213

**Customer:** Hi, I was charged twice for my Growth plan this month, can you
help me figure out why?

**Agent:** Hi! Sorry about that, let me take a look. Can you confirm the last
4 digits of the card and the approximate dates of the two charges?

**Customer:** Card ending 4471, charges on May 1st and May 3rd, both for $299.

**Agent:** I see it — looks like the May 1st charge was your regular monthly
renewal, and the May 3rd one was triggered when you added the "Advanced
Fraud Rules" add-on mid-cycle, which is billed immediately and prorated
separately rather than folded into the next renewal. That's expected
behavior, not a duplicate charge, but I understand it can look like one on a
statement.

**Customer:** Ah okay that makes sense, thanks. One more thing — can I get an
itemized receipt showing both separately?

**Agent:** Yep, I've just emailed you an itemized statement breaking out both
charges. You can also always pull these yourself from Dashboard > Billing >
Invoices going forward.

**Customer:** Perfect, thank you!

**Resolution:** Not a duplicate charge — regular renewal + prorated add-on
charge. Itemized receipt sent. Ticket closed.
