# Pricing Plans

| Plan | Monthly base fee | Per-transaction fee | Included seats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0 | 2.9% + $0.30 | 2 |
| Growth | $299 | 2.5% + $0.25 | 10 |
| Enterprise | Custom (negotiated) | Custom, typically 1.8%-2.2% | Unlimited |

## Plan Feature Comparison

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| API rate limit | 25 req/s | 100 req/s | Negotiated, typically 500+ req/s |
| Webhook retries | Standard schedule | Standard schedule | Custom retry schedule available |
| SLA uptime | None | 99.9% | 99.95% |
| Support | Community + email | Priority email, 1 business day | Dedicated Slack channel, 15-min Sev-1 |
| SSO | Not available | Not available | Available (add-on `ADDON-SSO-001`) |

## Annual Discount

Annual billing on Growth and Enterprise plans receives a 15% discount versus
monthly billing, paid upfront.

See `enterprise-addons-catalog.md` for optional add-ons available to
Enterprise customers.
