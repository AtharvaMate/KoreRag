# Enterprise Add-Ons Catalog

| SKU | Add-on | Price | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADDON-SSO-001 | SSO / SAML | $500/mo | Enterprise SSO via SAML 2.0, supports Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace |
| ADDON-FRAUD-002 | Advanced Fraud Rules | $299/mo | Custom risk-scoring rules beyond the default risk engine |
| ADDON-AUDIT-003 | Extended Audit Logs | $199/mo | Extends audit log retention from 90 days to 3 years |
| ADDON-SUPPORT-004 | Dedicated TAM | $1,500/mo | Named Technical Account Manager, monthly business reviews |
| ADDON-REGION-005 | Multi-Region Failover | $800/mo | Active-active deployment across two cloud regions for the customer's traffic |
| ADDON-WEBHOOK-006 | Custom Webhook Retry Schedule | $150/mo | Configurable retry intervals and max-retry count beyond the standard schedule |

## Provisioning

Add-ons are provisioned by the solutions engineering team after a signed
order form is received; typical provisioning time is 2-3 business days,
except ADDON-REGION-005 (Multi-Region Failover) which requires 2-3 weeks due
to infrastructure setup.

## Bundling

`ADDON-SSO-001` and `ADDON-AUDIT-003` are bundled at a 10% discount when
purchased together as part of the "Enterprise Security Bundle."
