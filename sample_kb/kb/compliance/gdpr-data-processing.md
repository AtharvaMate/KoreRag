# GDPR Data Processing — Internal Reference

## Legal Basis for Processing

Northwind Cloud processes merchant and end-customer data under the following
legal bases depending on data type:

- Transaction data: contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b))
- Fraud/risk scoring data: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f))
- Marketing communications: consent (Article 6(1)(a))

## Data Processing Agreements

All EU/UK customers on Growth and Enterprise plans are covered by a standard
Data Processing Agreement (DPA), auto-executed at signup. Starter plan
customers must request a DPA manually via legal@northwindcloud.com.

## Subprocessors

Current approved subprocessors include our cloud infrastructure provider, our
card network partners, and our email delivery provider. The full subprocessor
list is maintained at trust.northwindcloud.com and must be updated within 30
days of onboarding any new subprocessor, per contractual notice obligations.

## Erasure Requests

Erasure requests (GDPR Article 17) are routed through the internal privacy
tooling, which cross-references the `data-retention-policy.md` exceptions
before executing deletion. Requests involving open financial disputes are
placed on hold until the dispute resolves.
