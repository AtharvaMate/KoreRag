# Northwind Cloud — Product Overview

Northwind Cloud is a B2B payments and billing infrastructure platform. It lets
companies accept payments, manage recurring subscriptions, reconcile invoices,
and issue refunds through a single unified API.

## Core Modules

- **Payments API** — process one-time charges, holds, and captures across cards,
  ACH, and wallets.
- **Billing Engine** — recurring subscriptions, proration, dunning, and invoicing.
- **Webhooks** — real-time event delivery for payment and subscription lifecycle
  events.
- **Ledger** — double-entry accounting record of every money movement, used for
  reconciliation and financial reporting.
- **Risk Engine** — real-time fraud scoring on every transaction.

## Supported Regions

Northwind Cloud currently supports merchants domiciled in the United States,
Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. Support for APAC merchants
is on the 2027 roadmap.
