# Refund Policy (v2 — CURRENT, effective 2026-01-01 onward)

This is the current refund policy. It replaces the v1 policy effective
2025-12-31.

## Refund Window

- **Starter and Growth plans:** full refund within **30 days** of a charge.
- **Enterprise plans:** refunds are governed by the negotiated terms in the
  customer's signed order form; the default contractual window is **45 days**
  unless otherwise specified.

## Annual Plan Cancellations

Annual plans canceled before the renewal date are refunded on a **prorated**
basis for the unused portion of the term, **minus a 5% early-termination
administrative fee**, introduced in this version.

## Non-Refundable Items

Setup fees and one-time professional services engagements are non-refundable
once work has commenced.

## Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within **5-7 business days**, down from 10
business days under the prior policy, due to a payment-processor upgrade
completed in Q4 2025.
