# Data Retention Policy

## Transaction Records

Payment and refund records are retained for **7 years** to satisfy financial
and tax regulations across all supported regions.

## Customer PII

Personally identifiable information collected during onboarding (name,
address, government ID for KYC) is retained for the duration of the customer
relationship plus **5 years** after account closure, then permanently deleted.

## Support Tickets

Support ticket transcripts, including any attachments, are retained for
**3 years** and then automatically purged.

## Webhook Logs

Raw webhook delivery logs are retained for **90 days** for debugging purposes,
after which only aggregate delivery-success metrics are kept.

## Right to Erasure

EU/UK customers may request erasure of their PII under GDPR Article 17 subject
to the financial-record retention exceptions above. See
`gdpr-data-processing.md` for the full process.
