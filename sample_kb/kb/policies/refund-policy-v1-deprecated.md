# Refund Policy (v1 — DEPRECATED, effective through 2025-12-31)

> **This version is deprecated.** It is kept in the knowledge base for
> historical reference only. Do not quote these terms to current customers —
> see `refund-policy-v2-current.md` for the policy in effect today.

## Refund Window

Customers on any plan may request a full refund within **14 days** of a
charge, no questions asked.

## Annual Plan Cancellations

Annual plans canceled before the renewal date are refunded on a **prorated**
basis for the unused portion of the term.

## Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within **10 business days**.
